# Create a task

The exact interface can change, so use the scheduled-task controls currently available in your ChatGPT account. The underlying planning process remains the same.

## 1. Start with a recurring activity

Avoid beginning with a vague goal such as "help me with my career". Identify a concrete activity instead:

> Every Monday, find newly announced opportunities relevant to my project and summarise the strongest matches.

This gives the task a defined purpose and a natural cadence.

## 2. Specify the schedule

Choose a schedule that matches how quickly the information changes and how often you can act on it.

Examples:

- Daily — monitor information that changes frequently.
- Weekly — review opportunities, progress or recurring updates.
- Monthly — produce a periodic review or summary.
- One-time — prepare a reminder or a piece of work for a particular date.

Do not schedule a task more frequently than you can meaningfully review its results.

## 3. Define the expected output

A useful instruction says what the result should contain. For example:

- A short summary of new findings.
- Links to primary sources.
- Dates and eligibility criteria where relevant.
- A clear explanation when nothing meaningful has changed.

You can also specify what should be excluded. Exclusions are often as important as inclusion criteria because they reduce irrelevant results.

## 4. Review the first run

After the task runs, check:

- Is the information relevant?
- Are the sources current and credible?
- Is the output concise enough to use?
- Are important constraints being respected?
- Is the task repeating information you already know?

Update the instructions rather than accepting an unhelpful result as inevitable.

## 5. Maintain the task

A scheduled task is not a set-and-forget system. Review it when your goals, constraints or the external environment change. Pause or remove tasks that no longer provide value.

## Before you schedule

A quick test:

- [ ] I can describe the task in one sentence.
- [ ] I know how often I need the result.
- [ ] I have defined what counts as relevant.
- [ ] I have described the output I want.
- [ ] I know what I will do with the result.
